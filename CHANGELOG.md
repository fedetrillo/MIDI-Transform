# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

- First release: six tools for the MIDI notes selected in LUNA, or every note in a selected
  range of MIDI clips on one or more tracks.
- **Quantize:** 1/4 to 1/32 in six feels (straight, triplet, dotted, quintuplet, septuplet and
  mixed straight + triplet), with strength, swing, catch range, free zone and note ends.
- **Humanize:** small random changes to timing (in ms), velocity and length; notes of a chord
  move together; roll again for a new take.
- **Velocity:** scale (squeeze or spread the dynamics) with offset, a fixed value, or a ramp
  (crescendo or decrescendo) with a curve.
- **Length:** legato with a gap, a fixed length, or a percentage.
- **Pitch:** transpose by semitones and octaves, force to a scale (key read from LUNA, eight
  scales), or invert around the lowest note, the centre or the highest note.
- **Time:** reverse, double speed, half speed (the clip grows if there is room) or shift by
  grid steps.
- Before and after preview with a velocity lane; each Apply is one undo step, selected notes
  stay selected, and muted notes stay muted.
- Dark and grey themes, seven colours, 7 languages, Alt-click the version for a support report.
